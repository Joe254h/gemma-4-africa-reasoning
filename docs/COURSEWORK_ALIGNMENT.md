# Coursework alignment

The supplied AIMS coursework informed the project structure without copying course files into this repository.

## Generative and Agentic AI material

- `cours_aims_module0_session1.pdf` and `session2.pdf`: transformer and attention foundations.
- `cours_aims_module1_session3.pdf`: the LLM pipeline from pretraining to supervised adaptation and alignment.
- `cours_aims_module1_session4.pdf`: model selection, reasoning, quantization, cost, and African deployment contexts.
- `cours_aims_module2_session7.pdf`: LoRA/QLoRA adaptation under limited compute.
- `cours_aims_module2_session8.pdf`: evaluation metrics, rubrics, and the principle of measuring before trusting.
- `cours_aims_module4_session13_synthesis.pdf`: defending engineering choices through a coherent project workflow.

## Natural Language Processing material

- `7-Seq2seq-MT-models-part2.pdf`: translation and attention concepts relevant to reasoning-trace translation.
- `8-Transformers-1.pdf` and `8-Transformers-2.pdf`: transformer architecture and decoding.
- `10-LLM.pdf`: prompting, generation, fine-tuning, alignment, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning.

## How the learning appears in this repository

- architecture choice is separated from evaluation evidence;
- the base model is frozen for later parameter-efficient training;
- prompts, decoding, and dataset revisions are controlled experimental variables;
- evaluation combines objective metrics with a human rubric;
- low-resource language quality is reviewed rather than assumed;
- deployment optimization is postponed until the model's core behavior is demonstrated.

The coursework PDFs remain local source material and are not redistributed.
